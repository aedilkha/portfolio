VOGUE - Fragment of the Soul

I wanted to make a fashion VOGUE style drawing of someone I deeply love. Her particular gaze and fractured hands reveal emotions words cannot express. Each scare carries the weight of battles she fought in silence, reminding that true beauty is not found in perfection, but in the courage to keep moving forward.
